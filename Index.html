CSR / Sponsorship / Donation Policy

1. Purpose

This policy outlines the guidelines and procedures for Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR), sponsorship, and donation requests within Cahya Mata Sarawak Group...

2. Scope

This policy applies to all requests for CSR support, sponsorships, and donations received by the Group...

3. Eligibility Criteria

Requests must meet the following criteria:

  • Aligned with the Group's CSR focus areas
  • Demonstrate clear social or environmental impact
  • Have proper documentation and registration
  • Not for personal gain or political purposes

4. Focus Areas

  • Education and Youth Development
  • Community Well-Being and Development
  • Environmental Conservation
  • Indigenous Culture and Heritage
  • Sports and Recreation
  • Health and Safety

5. Approval Process

All requests will go through the following approval chain based on monetary value:

  • Up to RM 5,000: CSR Team → Head of GCC
  • RM 5,001 - RM 1,000,000: CSR Team → Head of GCC → GCFO
  • Above RM 1,000,000: Full approval chain including GMD

6. Documentation Requirements

  • Formal written request
  • Organization registration certificate
  • Banking details for payment
  • Event proposal or project details
  • Previous engagement history (if applicable)

7. Prohibited Activities

The Group will not support:

  • Political campaigns or activities
  • Religious propagation
  • Personal financial assistance
  • Activities that may harm the Group's reputation
  • Recurring requests without proper justification

8. Payment Terms

All approved contributions will be paid via bank transfer to the beneficiary's verified account. Cash payments are strictly prohibited.

9. Reporting and Accountability

Beneficiaries must provide:

  • Acknowledgement of receipt
  • Post-event report with photos
  • Evidence of fund utilization
  • Impact statement

10. SDG Alignment

All CSR activities should align with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) to ensure our contributions support global sustainability efforts.

11. Review and Updates

This policy will be reviewed annually and updated as necessary to reflect changing business priorities and community needs.

Acknowledgement

By proceeding with this CSR request, I acknowledge that:

  • I have read and understood this policy in its entirety
  • The information provided in my request is accurate and complete
  • I will comply with all documentation and reporting requirements
  • I understand the approval process and timelines
  • Any misrepresentation may result in rejection or withdrawal of support
